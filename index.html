About Us

Project name: Campus Canteen Management System

Purpose: To demonstrate digital food ordering and basic canteen account management.

A college canteen serves students and teachers every day. This demo lets users browse food, add items to a cart, and pay from a prepaid wallet, with optional Udhari (credit) for the remaining amount.

This is a student project for local demonstration only. The wallet is simulated and no real money is involved.
